# say-notify — phrase reference & inspiration bank

Reference for [`say-notify.sh`](./say-notify.sh) (the Claude Code Notification hook
that speaks aloud via macOS `say`). Two parts:

1. **What the live hook says now** — the concise radio format actually in use.
2. **Inspiration bank** — the full collected movie/game/military phrase library,
   grouped by source. These are *shelved* (not spoken verbatim); the live bodies are
   *inspired by* them. Pull from here when adding or re-theming bodies.

`~/Desktop/say-notify-phrases.md` is a symlink to this file. Central is canonical.

---

## 1. Live format (current)

```
[soft comms beep]  "<opener with callsign>. <body>"
```

- **Beep** = a soft 660 Hz double-blip (`say-notify-beep.wav`, generated by
  `say-notify-beep.py`) `afplay`ed before the line. Chosen by ear as non-grating for
  repeated use; tweak pitch/softness in the generator, not the wav.
- **Callsign** = `"<Project> <n>"` — project from cwd basename (first segment, no
  trailing digits, capitalized), instance numbered per-project via a session registry.
  Spoken with spaces (say reads dashes aloud). e.g. *Muser 1, Notes 1, Bas 2*.
- **Openers:** `Godfather, this is {cs}.` · `{cs} to Godfather.` · `{cs} here.` · `Godfather, {cs}.`
- **Bodies**, routed by notification type:
  - **idle / waiting-for-input** (the one that fires in bypass-permissions mode):
    over to you · come in · talk to me · do you copy? · you're up · you're on deck ·
    holding for you · how do you read? · standing by · awaiting your orders ·
    say your intentions · radio check · wake me when you need me · what's your status? ·
    sitrep? · send it · go ahead · say the word · your move · your orders? · sound off ·
    I'm all ears
  - **mutating/exec tool permission** (`{detail}` = command head / generic verb, never a filename):
    danger close, {detail}? · weapons hot, {detail}? · cleared hot to {detail}? ·
    go on {detail}? · {detail}, danger close? · {detail}, weapons free? · clear to engage {detail}?
  - **read-only/benign tool permission:**
    cleared to {detail}? · go for {detail}? · permission to {detail}? · okay to {detail}? ·
    {detail}, your go? · you are go for {detail}? · {detail}, cleared hot?
