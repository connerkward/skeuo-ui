Conclusion

NEUTRAL-TO-MILDLY-HELPFUL, not a fix. The fenced-JSON encoding did not hurt anything measured, and directionally improved the two residue axes: guide-hue bleed was lower in the treatment in 4/4 paired gens (mean 5.04% -> 1.56%; a 4/4 sign at n=4 is p=0.0625 one-sided — directional, not significant), and the extract12 gate passed 3/4 treat vs 2/4 control (both control FAILs were emptiness; one treat FAIL was region-misplaced+emptiness). It does NOT fix the two dominant paint defects: layout drift is equally bad in both arms (mean 690px control vs 641px treat on a ~2300px canvas — both treat the locked template as a suggestion), and guide-hue button echo still occurred in a treat gen (fa-pod-treat-134). Encoding the spec as JSON does not make the image model obey it; the JSON block mainly seems to reduce how often the guide COLOUR words get painted as pigment. Recommendation: do not adopt as a fix; if adopted at all, it is safe (no observed cost) — but the layout-adherence and colour-echo levers remain key selection, icon-no-echo clauses, and mask-column decoupling, not prompt encoding. n=4 pairs; single roll per cell.
