Honest notes: fal has NO dedicated delighting/intrinsic-decomposition or emissive-decomposition model — fal-ai/patina is the closest (single-image → 5 PBR maps) and its basecolor IS a delight pass. It delit the diablo stone/iron well (shadows+glow largely stripped, pixel-aligned) but barely delit vario's glossy dark plastic (rim glow + screen streaks survive). The gemini flat-prompt albedo kept photorealism on diablo but went flat/vector on vario — the exact cel failure it was prompted against. Emissive decomposition had to be classical (HSV chroma×luminance windows) — it is clean on both skins. The procedural glass env is a gradient+streak fake: it reads convincingly while the light moves, but breaks down on close inspection (no true scene reflection, no refraction, and the de-bake flattens legitimate screen content along with the baked streaks).
