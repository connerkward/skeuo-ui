knob-zero fix — proof

What was wrong: the model paints the knob's pointer/notch at whatever angle it feels like on the cut cap sprite, but the player rotated the cap relative to that raw cut — so init/value-0.5 showed the pointer at its arbitrary baked angle instead of straight up. What the pipeline does now: extract12.py's detect_knob_zero_deg() finds the painted indicator as a local radial anomaly in the cap's own gradient-magnitude angular profile (robust z-score vs. the cap's radially-symmetric body, rejecting wide specular-highlight humps) and writes regions[knob].knob_zero_deg; build_player.py counter-rotates the cap by that angle so value 0.5 always reads 12 o'clock, regardless of the raw cut's baked orientation.
