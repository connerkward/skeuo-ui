Lit icon — the GLYPH is masked out and lit, not the whole button

The glyph is extracted from the button by luminance-keying it out of the body, then lit (accent + white-hot core, optional bloom clipped to the button silhouette). OFF = the plain baked button. Click to toggle OFF↔ON. The "glyph mask" cell shows exactly which pixels were isolated as the icon. No rim, no ring; any glow stays inside the button.
