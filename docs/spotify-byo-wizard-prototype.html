skeuo

Connect your Spotify

A one-time, ~90-second setup so your skins can control your real Spotify. You make your own free Spotify “app” — skeuo never sees your password.

1 Float the helper

Pop out an always-on-top setup helper

It floats a small window with everything to copy — and an “Open Spotify dashboard” button inside it. Open the dashboard from there and the helper stays on top of it, so you copy → paste without ever switching tabs. (Browsers only allow one new window per click, so we float first, then open the dashboard from the float.)

Why: Spotify caps any one app at ~5 users, so skeuo can’t connect you on its shared app. Your own app makes you the owner — no waitlist, unlimited skins.
